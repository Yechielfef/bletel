About the Bletel

The Community’s Weekly Advertiser

The Bletel is the only free Yiddish-language, magazine-style publication dedicated to the heimishe community, home delivered to thousands of families each week across Montreal and Tosh and available at newsstands: local news, community updates and engaging content that has become a staple in our readers’ homes.

More than a publication, the Bletel is a trusted space where readers help one another, share simchos and stay connected, like a community chat in print. For many, Shabbos simply doesn’t start without it.
